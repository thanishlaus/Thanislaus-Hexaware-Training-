# Agile Meetings Documentation

## Overview

Agile meetings, also known as Agile ceremonies, help teams plan, track progress, review completed work, and continuously improve throughout the software development lifecycle.

---

## Agile Meetings

### 1. Sprint Planning

**Purpose:**
Plan the work to be completed in the upcoming sprint.

**Participants:**
- Product Owner
- Scrum Master
- Development Team

**Output:**
- Sprint Goal
- Sprint Backlog

---

### 2. Daily Scrum (Stand-Up Meeting)

**Purpose:**
Track daily progress and identify blockers.

**Duration:**
15 Minutes

**Discussion Points:**
- What did I complete yesterday?
- What will I do today?
- Are there any blockers?

**Participants:**
- Scrum Master
- Development Team

---

### 3. Sprint Review

**Purpose:**
Demonstrate completed work and collect stakeholder feedback.

**Participants:**
- Product Owner
- Scrum Master
- Development Team
- Stakeholders

**Output:**
- Feedback
- Updated Product Backlog

---

### 4. Sprint Retrospective

**Purpose:**
Review team performance and identify improvements.

**Discussion Points:**
- What went well?
- What can be improved?
- Action items for next sprint

**Participants:**
- Scrum Master
- Development Team

---

## Agile Meeting Flow

```text
Sprint Planning
       ↓
Daily Scrum
       ↓
Sprint Review
       ↓
Sprint Retrospective
       ↓
Next Sprint
```

---

## Benefits of Agile Meetings

- Better Team Collaboration
- Improved Communication
- Faster Issue Resolution
- Continuous Feedback
- Increased Productivity
- Better Quality Software
- Continuous Improvement
---

## Key Learning Outcome

Understanding Agile meetings helps teams effectively plan, execute, review, and improve software development activities while delivering value to customers continuously.
